§1 Point of Sale (POS) — Billing & Checkout

✅ Core POS Features

  • Real-time product search: Search by name or category, filters live as you type
  • Barcode/QR scanner support: USB HID scanner emulates keyboard input (plug & play, no drivers)
  • 6-column product grid: Display products with emoji/image, batch-linked selling price, and stock status (color-coded)
  • Shopping cart (persistent): Cart items remain after completing a sale — you can keep selling OR clear manually with red button
  • Editable quantity & price: Click quantity or price in cart to edit on-the-fly. Qty edits have stock limits. Price edits show strikethrough for original

🧮 Batch-Aware Pricing (New)

  • Oldest-batch price display: Product grid pulls selling price from the oldest available batch (FIFO order), not a fixed product price
  • Automatic fallback: If no batch exists for a product, the product's base sellingPrice is used instead
  • Cart consistency: When added to cart, each item locks in the batch-derived price at the moment of adding

💳 Payment & Checkout

  • Full payment (cash only): Enter amount, automatic change calculation
  • Partial payment with customer credit: If customer linked: pay less than total → remaining goes to customer's udhar. Error only shown if NO customer linked
  • Zero amount entry (full credit): No amount entered + customer linked = full bill becomes udhar
  • Optional customer linking: Button "Link to Customer (Udhar/Credit)" opens customer picker with favorites at top
  • Auto stock deduction: Stock reduces after every sale instantly, consumed from the oldest batch first (FIFO)

📄 Receipt Generation

  • Invoice design: Shop name (custom font, always black & bold 900), Invoice badge, address, phone, date/time
  • Item table: Description, Price, Qty, Total (with bold toggle for items)
  • Discount display: Shows discount % and amount with bold toggle option
  • Custom labels: "Credit (Udhar)" text is customizable in settings (can be "Baqi", "Remaining", anything)
  • Bold credit label option: When ON: 17px font, 900 weight, red border separator (like total line). When OFF: 13px, normal weight
  • Terms & Conditions: Optional T&C block at bottom with bold toggle
  • Footer message: Custom shop sign-off (custom font, always bold 900, black)
  • Print button: Opens print dialog, receipt formatted for thermal printer (80mm width)

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Interactions

  • F11: Toggle fullscreen mode (useful for kiosk setup)
  • Barcode input: Green-bordered field auto-focuses, Enter-key triggers product add
  • Click quantity: Turns into editable input (starts empty, shows current as placeholder)
  • Click price: Turns into editable input for custom pricing
  • Escape key: Cancels qty/price edits, reverts to previous value
  • Sliding cart: ChevronRight in cart header hides it. Green cart button appears in search bar to show it again

§3 FIFO Batch Tracking (New in v1.0.12)

✨ Why Batch Tracking

  • Products purchased at different times often have different cost and selling prices
  • FIFO (First-In-First-Out) ensures the oldest stock is sold first — matching real retail practice, especially for stock with limited shelf life
  • Each sale's profit is calculated against the actual cost of the batch it was drawn from, not an average or a single fixed cost

📥 Adding a Batch

  • Batch fields: Product, quantity, cost price, selling price, purchase date, optional expiry date, batch number, vendor name, notes
  • Auto stock sync: Adding a batch automatically increases the linked product's total stock quantity
  • Multiple batches per product: A single product can have many active batches queued in purchase order

📤 FIFO Consumption on Sale

  • Oldest-first logic: When a sale is completed, quantity is deducted from the oldest batch with available stock first, then the next-oldest if the first runs out
  • Consumption record: Each deduction is logged — batch ID, product ID, quantity taken, cost price at time of sale, selling price, linked sale ID
  • Accurate profit: Profit per sale = Σ (selling price − actual batch cost price) × qty consumed from each batch

💰 Batch Selling Price on POS

  • The POS product grid displays the selling price of the oldest available batch — the one that will actually be sold next
  • Falls back to the product's base selling price if no batch stock remains

⚠️ Batch Deletion & Archiving (Planned)

  • Deleting a batch outright can break historical profit calculations if that batch was already sold from — its cost reference disappears from past sales
  • Safer options under consideration: archiving a depleted/unwanted batch (hides it from active inventory but keeps it for profit history), or a write-off function to reduce quantity for damage/expiry while preserving the cost trail
  • Only batches with zero remaining quantity and zero linked sales history are safe to fully delete
Sale of 8 units draws first from the oldest batch, then spills into the next batch — each portion keeps its own recorded cost.

§4 Inventory Tracking

📊 Stock Monitoring

  • Visual progress bars: Green bar shows current stock as % of warehouse capacity
  • Stock levels: Displays current qty, restock trigger threshold (configurable in settings)
  • Low stock alerts: Items below threshold highlighted in orange/red (visual alert), with a popup shown on the first bill after opening the app
  • Color coding: Green (sufficient), Yellow (approaching limit), Red (below threshold)

🔄 Restocking

  • Restock modal: Enter quantity to add, optional purchase cost (for tracking spending)
  • Batch-based restocking: Each restock can be entered as a new batch, preserving its own cost and selling price for FIFO
  • Auto-update: Stock updates instantly across all pages (POS, reports, products, batches)

📋 Inventory Reports

  • Stock summary: Total items, items in stock, low stock count
  • Average stock value: Total cost of inventory, calculated from live batch quantities
  • Stock turnover: Items sold this month vs avg sell rate

§5 Customer Management & Udhar (Credit) Tracking

✨ Customer Features

  • Complete customer profile system with purchase history & credit tracking
  • Support for partial payments and manual debt addition
  • Automatic pending amount calculation with formula transparency
  • Print customer statements for record-keeping

👥 Customer List & Management

  • Customer profiles: Name, phone, address (optional), favorite toggle
  • Favorite customers: Quick access at top of list, visible in POS customer picker
  • Search & filter: Search by name or phone number, filter favorites only
  • KPI row: Total customers, customers with pending udhar, total pending amount (all customers)
  • Customer card displays: Total spent, sales count, pending amount with color badge
  • Edit/Delete: Modify customer details or remove from system (sales remain in reports)

💰 Pending Amount (Udhar) Calculation

  • Formula: Pending = Total Billed − Amount Paid at Sale − Manual Payments + Manual Dues Added
  • Visual formula bar: Shows exact calculation on customer detail page for transparency
  • Color-coded status: Green (Clear), Yellow (<1000 pending), Red (≥1000 pending)
  • Automatic calculation: Recalculates after every payment/due entry
  • Display format: Shows amount in currency or "✓ Clear" if zero

📊 Customer Detail View

  • Profile header: Customer name, phone, address, customer since date, favorite star
  • KPI tiles (4): Total Billed, Total Received (paid at sale + manual payments), Dues Added, Pending (Udhar)
  • Alert banner: Red (if pending) or Green (if all paid) with action buttons
  • Two tabs: Purchase History & Payment Ledger

🧾 Purchase History Tab

  • All sales linked to customer: Date, time, transaction ID, items purchased, total, amount paid, status (Paid/Credit/Partial)
  • Discount display: Shows discount amount if any
  • Due tracking: Shows remaining due for each sale if not fully paid
  • Sorted by date: Most recent first

💳 Payment Ledger Tab (Combined)

  • Payment entries: Money received from customer (minus icon, green)
  • Due entries: Manual debts added (plus icon, orange)
  • Each entry shows: Date, time, amount, type (Payment/Due), notes, delete button
  • Timeline view: Sorted newest first, complete history of all transactions

➕ Record Payment & Add Due Buttons

  • Record Payment: Modal to enter amount received from customer, optional notes (e.g., "Paid full", "Partial")
  • Quick buttons: "Full" button (auto-fill pending amount), "Half" button (auto-fill 50%)
  • Add Manual Due: Modal to add debt not from a sale (old udhar, loan, etc.)
  • Both modals: Have note field for documenting reason/reference
  • Mode switcher: Tab switcher inside modal to toggle between Record Payment and Add Due

🖨 Print Customer Statement

  • Print button: Top-right of customer detail page
  • Statement includes: Customer name, phone, address, customer since date
  • Summary KPIs: Total billed, total received, manual dues added
  • Outstanding balance box: Red highlighted if pending, green highlighted if clear
  • Full tables: All purchases (with due status), all payment/due entries
  • Print formatting: A4 landscape, ready for direct print or PDF save

§6 Reports & Analytics

📊 Overview Tab

  • Period selector: Daily / Weekly / Monthly buttons to switch chart view
  • Product filter: Dropdown to filter reports by specific product
  • Revenue chart: Bar chart showing revenue for last 7 days (daily), 6 weeks (weekly), or 6 months (monthly)
  • Top Selling Products: Horizontal progress bars showing units sold (top 6 products)
  • Revenue by Category: Horizontal progress bars showing revenue per category (top 6)
  • Summary grid: Total sales, gross revenue, total discounts, net revenue, avg order value, total items sold

💵 FIFO-Accurate Profit

  • Profit figures pull from the batch consumption log (pharma_batch_consumptions) rather than a single fixed cost per product
  • Each sale's line item profit = (selling price − actual batch cost) summed across every batch portion consumed
  • Gives a realistic view of margins when purchase costs fluctuate between restocks

🧾 Transactions Tab

  • Transaction list: All sales as clickable cards showing TXN ID, date/time, items count, total, pending (if any)
  • Status badge: Green checkmark if paid, red clock if unpaid/pending
  • Customer tag: Shows customer name if linked (blue badge)
  • Action buttons: Eye (view details), Edit, Print, Delete

🔍 Filtering & Sorting

  • Product filter: Dropdown to show only sales of specific product
  • Date range: "From" and "To" date pickers (calendar inputs)
  • Clear all filters: Button to reset all filters at once
  • Count display: Shows filtered transaction count at top

👁 View Transaction Details

  • Modal popup: Shows full invoice layout (shop name, address, phone, date/time, items table, totals)
  • Item details: Product name, price per unit, quantity, line total
  • Calculation row: Sub-total, discount, total, paid amount, change, credit (if any)
  • Action buttons: Edit, Print, Delete right in the modal header

✏️ Edit Transaction

  • Edit fields: Discount % and Amount Paid only (items cannot be modified)

📥 Export & Reset

§8 System Architecture & Data Flow

React pages call into two store modules — general CRUD (db.ts) and FIFO batch logic (batch-fifo.ts) — both persisting to browser localStorage inside the Electron shell.
Every completed sale triggers FIFO batch consumption and, if under-paid with a linked customer, an automatic udhar entry.

📁 Project Structure

  • src/App.tsx: Root component with page routing (adds 'batches' route)
  • src/pages/: POSPage, ProductsPage, BatchesPage, InventoryPage, ReportsPage, SettingsPage, CustomersPage, CustomerDetailPage
  • src/components/: Sidebar navigation with collapsible menu
  • src/store/db.ts: Core database layer — products, sales, customers, payments, settings, backup/restore
  • src/store/batch-fifo.ts: FIFO batch engine — addProductBatch, consumeProductBatches, calculateProfitFIFO, getProductBatchSellingPrice
  • electron/main.cjs: Electron main process (F11 fullscreen, focus fix)
